Privacy Policy — Planetlings

Effective date: November 23, 2026
App: Planetlings: Paint the Planet (com.devcodeapps.planetlings)
Developer: DevCodeApps
Contact: info@devcode.cloud

Summary

Planetlings is a single-player game: you paint a small planet in matches against a computer-controlled opponent. There is no account, no chat and no online multiplayer. The game is free. It shows ads from Google AdMob, you can make optional purchases through Google Play, and it sends gameplay statistics to Google Analytics for Firebase, so the developer can see how the game is played and improve it. The developer runs no servers for Planetlings and never receives your name, e-mail address or anything else that identifies you. The only data that leaves your phone through the game goes to Google, for ads, the consent choice that goes with them, purchases and gameplay statistics, as described below. Where the law requires your consent (for example in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom), no ads are requested and no statistics are collected until you agree.

What the game stores on your device

Your progress is saved on your device, in the game's own storage folder: trophies and league, coins, unlocked cards, the cosmetic items you own and have equipped, daily quests, the login calendar and the free chest timer, match statistics, whether you have finished the tutorial, and your sound, music and vibration settings. It is not sent to the developer. Uninstalling the game or clearing its data deletes it.

If Android's backup is turned on for your phone, Android may include this save in your phone's backup to your Google account, so your progress can come back after a reinstall or on a new phone. Google handles that backup; the developer cannot see it.

Advertising: Google AdMob

Planetlings shows ads from Google AdMob, a third party, through Google's Mobile Ads SDK built into the game: full-screen ads between matches, and rewarded ads that you can choose to watch in exchange for an in-game bonus. Watching a rewarded ad is always optional.

According to Google's published data disclosure for that software, it automatically collects and shares the following data for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention:

  • IP address. It may be used to estimate the general location of your device.
  • Product interactions, such as app launches, taps and video views.
  • Diagnostic information about how the game and the ads software perform, such as launch time, hang rate and energy use.
  • Device and account identifiers: the Android advertising ID, the app set ID and, where applicable, other identifiers related to signed-in accounts on the phone.

Google uses this data to show ads, to measure how they perform and to prevent fraud, and says it encrypts the data in transit. Google handles it under the Google Privacy Policy; see also How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. The game never sends your name, e-mail address or anything else about you to the ads software, because it does not have any of it.

Gameplay statistics: Google Analytics for Firebase

The game uses Google Analytics for Firebase, a service from Google, to count how the game is played: for example how many players finish the tutorial, how matches end, which items are bought with coins and where purchases stop. The developer uses these counts to balance and improve the game, and sees them only as reports in Google's Firebase and Analytics consoles. They are not used to show you ads. The developer runs no server of its own to receive this data; Google processes it for the developer.

The game sends only fixed names, the names of game items and coarse ranges (for example "30-39" for the share of the planet you painted):

  • Tutorial: when it starts, and when it is finished or skipped, with how far you got.
  • Matches: when a ranked match starts and how it ended (win, loss, draw, quit, or left by closing the game), your league, the share of the planet you painted as a range, the trophies won or lost, your win streak and the number of matches you have played as ranges. Also when you reach a new league or unlock a card.
  • Daily rewards and the shop: login rewards and chests opened, daily quests claimed, when the shop or its coins page is opened and from where, and which item you bought with coins and its coin price.
  • Ads: when a full-screen ad is shown, and which rewarded ad you chose to watch (for example "double coins").
  • Purchases: when the starter pack is offered, when you start a purchase, which product it was and how it ended (bought, pending, cancelled, failed or restored).
  • Properties attached to the statistics: your league, whether you have No Ads, and whether you have ever made a purchase (yes or no).

Google's Analytics software also collects some data automatically. According to Google's published data disclosure for it, this includes: an app instance ID (a random ID Google generates for each installation of the game, used to count players); the Android advertising ID, if your consent choice and Android settings allow it; coarse location derived from the IP address; sessions, first launch and app updates; completed Google Play purchases, with the product and its price; and information about your device, such as its model, Android version and language.

Google keeps this event data for 14 months, the retention set in the game's Analytics settings, and then deletes it. Google says it encrypts the data in transit and passes it to no third parties except subprocessors that help provide the service. More information: Privacy and Security in Firebase and Firebase's Google Play data disclosure.

Your choices about ads and statistics

  • Consent form. Where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland), the game shows Google's consent form, made with Google's User Messaging Platform, when it starts. No ad is requested and no statistics are collected until your choice allows it; if you do not agree, nothing is collected. The Analytics consent settings (analytics storage, ad storage, ad user data and ad personalisation) follow the choices you make in the form. One choice covers ads and statistics, and you can change or withdraw it at any time under Privacy options in the game's settings.
  • Where the law does not require consent, statistics are collected from the first start, and there is no separate switch to turn them off. Clearing the game's data or reinstalling it gives the game a new app instance ID that is not connected to the old one.
  • Personalised ads. If you do not agree to personalised ads, Google can still show ads that are not based on your past activity. These still use data such as the IP address for measurement and fraud prevention.
  • Advertising ID. You can reset or delete the Android advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalisation, in your phone's Android settings.

Purchases

Optional purchases, such as coins, are sold and processed entirely by Google Play. The developer never sees your payment details. The game asks Google Play to confirm each purchase before it delivers it. Purchases buy cosmetic items and coins only. Google handles purchase information under the Google Privacy Policy.

Information the developer collects

Nothing that identifies you. Planetlings has no sign-in and does not ask for your name, e-mail address, contacts, location, photos, files, camera or microphone. What the developer sees are the gameplay statistics described above, as reports in Google Analytics; they are tied only to the random app instance ID, never to your name or e-mail address. The game has no crash-reporting service, and the developer has no server that receives data from it.

Permissions

  • Internet, network state and Wi-Fi state: used for ads, the consent form, purchases, gameplay statistics and Google Play's licence check. Matches themselves are played on your phone and work offline.
  • Google Play Billing (com.android.vending.BILLING): shows prices and completes purchases with Google Play.
  • Google Play licence check (com.android.vending.CHECK_LICENSE): lets Google Play confirm the game was installed from the store.
  • Advertising ID and Android ad services (com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID, ACCESS_ADSERVICES_AD_ID, ACCESS_ADSERVICES_ATTRIBUTION, ACCESS_ADSERVICES_TOPICS): added by Google's ads and Analytics software, so it can use the advertising ID and Android's ad measurement and ad topics, as described under "Advertising" and "Gameplay statistics" above.
  • Vibration: haptic feedback while you play (it can be switched off in the settings).
  • Wake lock and audio settings: keep the screen on during a match and play sound and music.

Deleting your data

There is no account, and the developer holds no data that identifies you.

  • The save on your device is deleted when you uninstall the game or clear its data in Android's settings.
  • A copy in your phone's Google backup, if there is one, is part of your Google account's backups and can be removed there.
  • Data collected by Google's ads software is held by Google. You can reset or delete the advertising ID in Android settings and use Google's own privacy tools through the Google Privacy Policy.
  • Gameplay statistics are held by Google for the developer's Analytics account and deleted automatically after 14 months. They are tied only to the game's app instance ID; clearing the game's data or reinstalling it starts a new one.
  • Purchase records are held by Google Play.

Questions about any of this: info@devcode.cloud.

Children

Planetlings is meant for players aged 13 and over. It is not directed at children under 13, and the developer does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone. If you believe a child under 13 is playing and have a question about it, write to info@devcode.cloud.

Changes

If a future version adds crash reports, sign-in, cloud saves or anything else that changes what is described here, or changes how ads or statistics work, this policy will be updated before that version is released and the effective date above will change. The November 23, 2026 version added the gameplay statistics (Google Analytics for Firebase).
