Privacy Policy — Voltball

Effective date: November 18, 2026
App: Voltball (com.devcodeapps.voltball)
Developer: DevCodeApps
Contact: under.cntrl.dev@gmail.com

Summary

Voltball is a single-player arcade game. You play against the computer; there is no chat and no online multiplayer. The game has no account of its own. If you are signed in to Google Play Games, a copy of your progress is kept in your own Google account so that it comes back after a reinstall or on a new phone. The developer does not receive, store or share any personal information about you.

What the game stores on your device

Your progress (match results, stars, rank, coins, gems, unlocked items, a record of the purchases already paid out to you, and your sound, music and vibration settings) is saved on your device, in the game's private storage. Uninstalling the game or clearing its data deletes this copy.

Google Play Games: sign-in and cloud save

Voltball uses Google Play Games Services, a service run by Google.

  • Sign-in. If you have a Play Games profile, Google Play Games may sign you in when the game starts, or you can sign in from the game's settings (the Cloud Save row). Signing in is optional: the game works without it. Google handles the sign-in. The game does not read, store or send your name, e-mail address, Play Games name, avatar or player ID.
  • Cloud save. While you are signed in, the game uploads a copy of the progress described above to Google Play Games ("Saved Games"), where it is stored in your own Google account. The game reads it back when it starts, so your progress and purchased items return after a reinstall or on another device. This copy is sent over an encrypted connection. The developer cannot see or access it.
  • Google's own data. Google's Play Games software inside the game also collects analytics and diagnostics data for Google, to keep its service stable. See the Google Privacy Policy.
  • Not signed in. If you are not signed in to Google Play Games, nothing is uploaded and your progress stays only on your device.

Deleting your data

The cloud save (your game progress and the record of purchases paid out to you) belongs to your Google account, and you can delete it yourself: open the Google Play Games app (or your Google Account's Play Games settings), go to Settings → Delete Play Games account & data, and delete the data for Voltball. Your Play Games identity is part of your Google account and is deleted the same way. Uninstalling the game does not delete the cloud save.

The copy on your device is deleted when you uninstall the game or clear its data in Android's settings.

You can also write to under.cntrl.dev@gmail.com to ask about deleting your data and we will guide you through these steps. The developer holds no copy of your data, so there is nothing kept on the developer's side.

Information the developer collects

None. The developer runs no servers for Voltball and receives no data from the game. Voltball does not ask for your name, e-mail address, contacts, location, photos or files. It contains no advertising software, and the developer runs no analytics in it.

Purchases

If you buy something in the game, the purchase is handled entirely by Google Play. The developer never sees your payment details. Google's handling of your purchase information is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Permissions

  • Internet / network state: used for Google Play purchases and licensing, and for Google Play Games sign-in and the cloud save. The game itself can be played offline.
  • Vibration: haptic feedback during matches (it can be switched off in the settings).
  • Wake lock / audio settings: keep the screen on and play sound while you are in a match.

Children

Voltball is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes

If a future version adds advertising, analytics or anything else that changes what is described here, this policy will be updated first and the effective date above will change. November 18, 2026: Google Play Games sign-in and the cloud save were added.
