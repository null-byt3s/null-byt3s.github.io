Privacy Policy — Voltball

Effective date: November 16, 2026
App: Voltball (com.devcodeapps.voltball)
Developer: DevCodeApps
Contact: under.cntrl.dev@gmail.com

Summary

Voltball is a single-player arcade game. You play against the computer; there are no accounts, no chat and no online multiplayer. The developer does not collect, store or share any personal information about you.

What the game stores

Your progress (match results, stars, rank, coins, gems, unlocked items and your sound, music and vibration settings) is saved only on your device, in the game's private storage. It is not sent to the developer or to anyone else. Uninstalling the game or clearing its data deletes it.

Information the developer collects

None. Voltball has no sign-in, does not ask for your name, e-mail address, contacts, location, photos or files, and contains no analytics or advertising software.

Purchases

If you buy something in the game, the purchase is handled entirely by Google Play. The developer never sees your payment details. Google's handling of your purchase information is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Permissions

  • Internet / network state: used for Google Play purchases and licensing. The game itself can be played offline.
  • Vibration: haptic feedback during matches (it can be switched off in the settings).
  • Wake lock / audio settings: keep the screen on and play sound while you are in a match.

Children

Voltball is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes

If a future version adds advertising, analytics or anything else that changes what is described here, this policy will be updated first and the effective date above will change.
