Privacy Policy — Remain Below

Effective date: November 25, 2026
App: Remain Below: Bunker Escape (com.devcodeapps.remainbelow)
Developer: DevCodeApps
Contact: info@devcode.cloud

Summary

Remain Below is a single-player escape-room game: you explore a sealed bunker room by room, solve its puzzles and find the way out. There is no chat and no online multiplayer, and the game never asks for your name or e-mail address. It uses five services from Google, each described below: ads (Google AdMob), gameplay statistics (Google Analytics for Firebase), crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics), optional purchases (Google Play) and an optional cloud save with achievements (Google Play Games).

What the game stores on your device

Your progress (the room you are in, the rooms you have reached, what you have found and solved, the items you carry, your health, the hints you have seen and the time played), your hint coins and a record of the purchases already delivered, the achievements you have earned, and your sound settings are saved on your device, in the game's own storage folder. Uninstalling the game or clearing its data deletes them. If you use the cloud save (below), a copy of your progress and coins is also kept in your Google account.

Advertising: Google AdMob

Remain Below shows ads from Google AdMob, a third party, through Google's Mobile Ads SDK built into the game:

  • Rewarded ads that you can choose to watch for a hint coin, or to continue after the bunker gets you. Watching one is always optional.
  • Full-screen ads when you move from one room to another and when you choose RETRY after a death: never in the first room, never during a countdown, and at most one every three minutes. The optional "Remove ads" purchase turns these off.

According to Google's published data disclosure for that software, it automatically collects and shares the following data for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention:

  • IP address. It may be used to estimate the general location of your device.
  • Product interactions, such as app launches, taps and video views.
  • Diagnostic information about how the game and the ads software perform, such as launch time, hang rate and energy use.
  • Device and account identifiers: the Android advertising ID, the app set ID and, where applicable, other identifiers related to signed-in accounts on the phone.

Google uses this data to show ads, to measure how they perform and to prevent fraud, and says it encrypts the data in transit. Google handles it under the Google Privacy Policy; see also How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. The game never sends your name, e-mail address or anything else about you to the ads software, because it does not have any of it. Ads are limited to those Google rates suitable for general audiences.

Gameplay statistics: Google Analytics for Firebase

The game uses Google Analytics for Firebase, a service from Google, to count how the game is played: for example how many players reach each room, which puzzle step they spend longest on, how often they take a hint and where they stop. The developer uses these counts to make the puzzles fairer and the game better, and sees them only as reports in Google's Firebase and Analytics consoles. They are not used to show you ads. The developer runs no server of its own to receive this data; Google processes it for the developer.

The game sends only fixed names, the names of rooms, puzzles and items, small counts and coarse ranges. It never sends anything you type, such as the codes you try on a keypad:

  • Progress: each room reached and each puzzle step reached or solved, with the minutes played; items combined; the end of the episode, with the minutes played and the number of hints used.
  • Hints: when the hints are opened, which hint level was shown and what it cost in coins.
  • Hazards: when a countdown was stopped in time or ran out, when the player died (the room and the camera view, not the description) and whether they chose CONTINUE or RETRY.
  • Coins, ads and purchases: when coins were earned or spent and why, with the new balance as a range; when the shop is opened and from where; which product was chosen and how the purchase ended; which kind of ad was shown and how it ended.
  • Properties attached to the statistics: whether "Remove ads" is owned, and whether the game is a test build or the released one.

Google's Analytics software also collects some data automatically. According to Google's published data disclosure for it, this includes: an app instance ID (a random ID Google generates for each installation of the game, used to count players); the Android advertising ID, if your consent choice and Android settings allow it; coarse location derived from the IP address; sessions, first launch and app updates; how the game was installed from Google Play (the install referrer); completed Google Play purchases, with the product and its price; and information about your device, such as its model, Android version and language.

Google keeps this event data for up to 14 months and then deletes it. Google says it encrypts the data in transit and passes it to no third parties except subprocessors that help provide the service. More information: Privacy and Security in Firebase and Firebase's Google Play data disclosure.

Crash reports: Firebase Crashlytics

If the game crashes, Firebase Crashlytics, a service from Google, sends a crash report so the developer can find and fix the problem. A report contains technical information only: where in the game's code the crash happened, the game's version, the device model, its Android version, the available memory and storage, whether the game was in the foreground, and a random installation ID. It contains nothing you entered and nothing that identifies you. Google keeps crash reports for 90 days. More information: Privacy and Security in Firebase.

Your choices about ads and statistics

  • Consent form. Where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland), the game shows Google's consent form, made with Google's User Messaging Platform, when it starts. No ad is requested and no statistics are collected until your choice allows it; if you do not agree, nothing is collected. The Analytics consent settings (analytics storage, ad storage, ad user data and ad personalisation) follow the choices you make in the form. One choice covers ads and statistics, and you can change or withdraw it at any time with Privacy choices on the game's SETTINGS card (from the main menu or the pause button).
  • Where the law does not require consent, statistics are collected from the first start. Clearing the game's data or reinstalling it gives the game a new app instance ID that is not connected to the old one.
  • Personalised ads. If you do not agree to personalised ads, Google can still show ads that are not based on your past activity. These still use data such as the IP address for measurement and fraud prevention.
  • Advertising ID. You can reset or delete the Android advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalisation, in your phone's Android settings.
  • Remove ads. The optional "Remove ads" purchase stops the full-screen ads. Rewarded ads stay available, and you only see one when you choose to.

Google Play Games: sign-in, cloud save and achievements

Remain Below uses Google Play Games Services, a service run by Google.

  • Sign-in. If you have a Play Games profile, Google Play Games may sign you in when the game starts, or you can sign in from the game's SETTINGS card. Signing in is optional: the game works without it. Google handles the sign-in, and the game never sees your e-mail address.
  • Cloud save. While you are signed in, the game uploads a copy of your progress and your hint coins (with the record of purchases already delivered) to Google Play Games ("Saved Games"), where it is stored in your own Google account, together with the time it was saved and your phone's model name, so that you can tell copies apart. When the game finds a copy there that is further along than the one on your phone (after a reinstall or on another device), it asks you before loading it. This copy is sent over an encrypted connection. The developer cannot see or access it.
  • Achievements. While you are signed in, the achievements you earn are recorded in your Play Games profile by Google. Google's Play Games app shows them to you and, depending on your Play Games settings, to others.
  • Google's own data. Google's Play Games software inside the game also collects analytics and diagnostics data for Google, to keep its service stable. See the Google Privacy Policy.
  • Not signed in. If you are not signed in to Google Play Games, nothing is uploaded and your progress stays only on your device.

Purchases

Optional purchases (packs of hint coins and Remove ads) are sold and processed entirely by Google Play. The developer never sees your payment details. The game asks Google Play to confirm each purchase before it delivers it. Purchases buy in-game items only; they have no value outside the game. Google handles purchase information under the Google Privacy Policy.

Information the developer collects

Nothing that identifies you. Remain Below has no sign-in of its own and does not ask for your name, e-mail address, contacts, location, photos, files, camera or microphone. What the developer sees are the gameplay statistics and crash reports described above, as reports in Google's Firebase and Analytics consoles, tied only to random IDs, never to your name or e-mail address. The developer runs no server of its own that receives data from the game.

Permissions

  • Internet and network state: used for ads, the consent form, gameplay statistics, crash reports, purchases, the cloud save, achievements and Google Play's licence check. The bunker itself plays on your phone and works offline.
  • Google Play Billing (com.android.vending.BILLING): shows prices and completes purchases with Google Play.
  • Advertising ID and Android ad services (com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID and the ACCESS_ADSERVICES_* permissions): added by Google's ads and Analytics software, so it can use the advertising ID and Android's ad measurement, as described under "Advertising" and "Gameplay statistics" above.
  • Google Play install referrer: added by Google's Analytics software, to see how the game was installed from Google Play.
  • Wake lock and audio settings: keep the screen on while you play and play sound and music.
  • Vibration and foreground service: declared by the game engine by default. The game does not vibrate; the foreground service is only used by the engine to finish downloading the game's data, if needed.

Deleting your data

There is no account of the developer's, and the developer holds no data that identifies you.

  • The data on your device is deleted when you uninstall the game or clear its data in Android's settings.
  • The cloud save and achievements belong to your Google account, and you can delete them yourself: open the Google Play Games app (or your Google Account's Play Games settings), go to Settings → Delete Play Games account & data, and delete the data for Remain Below. Uninstalling the game does not delete them.
  • Gameplay statistics are held by Google for the developer's Analytics account and deleted automatically after at most 14 months; crash reports after 90 days. They are tied only to random IDs; clearing the game's data or reinstalling it starts new ones.
  • Data collected by Google's ads software is held by Google. You can reset or delete the advertising ID in Android settings and use Google's own privacy tools through the Google Privacy Policy.
  • Purchase records are held by Google Play.

Children

Remain Below is meant for players aged 13 and over. It is not directed at children under 13, and the developer does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone. If you believe a child under 13 is playing and have a question about it, write to info@devcode.cloud.
