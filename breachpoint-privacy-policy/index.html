Privacy Policy — Breachpoint

Effective date: November 17, 2026
App: Breachpoint (com.devcodeapps.breachpoint)
Developer: DevCodeApps
Contact: under.cntrl.dev@gmail.com

Summary

Breachpoint is a single-player strategy game. You play against computer-controlled networks; there are no accounts, no chat and no online multiplayer. The developer does not collect, store or share any personal information about you.

What the game stores

Your progress (your network and its machines, funds and crypto, programs, research, campaign jobs, daily contracts, rank, and your sound, music and vibration settings) is saved only on your device, in the game's own storage folder. It is not sent to the developer or to anyone else. Uninstalling the game or clearing its data deletes it.

Information the developer collects

None. Breachpoint has no sign-in, does not ask for your name, e-mail address, contacts, location, photos or files, and contains no analytics or advertising software.

Purchases

This version of the game sells nothing. If a future version offers in-app purchases, they will be handled entirely by Google Play; the developer never sees your payment details. Google's handling of purchase information is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Permissions

  • Internet / network and Wi-Fi state, Google Play licence check: used by Google Play to confirm the game was installed from the store. The game itself plays offline and sends nothing.
  • Vibration: haptic feedback during raids (it can be switched off in the settings).
  • Wake lock / audio settings: keep the screen on and play sound while you play.

Children

Breachpoint is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes

If a future version adds advertising, purchases, cloud saves, analytics or anything else that changes what is described here, this policy will be updated first and the effective date above will change.
