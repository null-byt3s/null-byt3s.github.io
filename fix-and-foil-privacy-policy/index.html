Privacy Policy — Fix & Foil

Effective date: November 24, 2026
App: Fix & Foil: Retro Shop Sim (com.devcodeapps.retroshop)
Developer: DevCodeApps
Contact: info@devcode.cloud

Summary

Fix & Foil is a single-player game: you run a small retro game and card shop, restore old machines and sell them to computer-controlled customers. There is no chat and no online multiplayer, and the game never asks for your name or e-mail address. It uses four services from Google, each described below: ads (Google AdMob), gameplay statistics (Google Analytics for Firebase), optional purchases (Google Play) and an optional cloud save (Google Play Games).

What the game stores on your device

Your shop (your money, shop level and upgrades, the things in your box, on your bench and on your shelves, your cards, your orders, the day, the tips you have seen, and what you have bought in the game) is saved on your device, in the game's own storage folder. Uninstalling the game or clearing its data deletes it. If you use the cloud save (below), a copy is also kept in your Google account.

Advertising: Google AdMob

Fix & Foil shows ads from Google AdMob, a third party, through Google's Mobile Ads SDK built into the game:

  • Rewarded ads that you can choose to watch for an in-game bonus (Rush hour, or a free crate of stock). Watching one is always optional.
  • A full-screen ad after a shop day's summary, from the fourth day on. The optional "Remove ads" purchase turns these off.

According to Google's published data disclosure for that software, it automatically collects and shares the following data for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention:

  • IP address. It may be used to estimate the general location of your device.
  • Product interactions, such as app launches, taps and video views.
  • Diagnostic information about how the game and the ads software perform, such as launch time, hang rate and energy use.
  • Device and account identifiers: the Android advertising ID, the app set ID and, where applicable, other identifiers related to signed-in accounts on the phone.

Google uses this data to show ads, to measure how they perform and to prevent fraud, and says it encrypts the data in transit. Google handles it under the Google Privacy Policy; see also How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. The game never sends your name, e-mail address or anything else about you to the ads software, because it does not have any of it. Ads are limited to those Google rates suitable for general audiences.

Gameplay statistics: Google Analytics for Firebase

The game uses Google Analytics for Firebase, a service from Google, to count how the game is played: for example how many players finish the first-time guide, how long it takes to reach each shop level and where players stop. The developer uses these counts to balance and improve the game, and sees them only as reports in Google's Firebase and Analytics consoles. They are not used to show you ads. The developer runs no server of its own to receive this data; Google processes it for the developer.

The game sends only fixed names, the names of game items and coarse ranges:

  • The first-time guide: how far you got.
  • Your shop: each shop level reached and the play time it took (as a range); at the end of each shop day, the day number, the takings as a range and how many things and cards were sold.
  • What you do in the shop: stock ordered (which lot), things restored (their kind and grade), machines repaired, card packs opened and upgrades bought.
  • Ads: which rewarded ad you chose to watch and how it ended, and when the ad after closing is shown.
  • Purchases: when the CASH & EXTRAS screen is opened, and which product you bought.

Google's Analytics software also collects some data automatically. According to Google's published data disclosure for it, this includes: an app instance ID (a random ID Google generates for each installation of the game, used to count players); the Android advertising ID, if your consent choice and Android settings allow it; coarse location derived from the IP address; sessions, first launch and app updates; completed Google Play purchases, with the product and its price; and information about your device, such as its model, Android version and language.

Google keeps this event data for up to 14 months and then deletes it. Google says it encrypts the data in transit and passes it to no third parties except subprocessors that help provide the service. More information: Privacy and Security in Firebase and Firebase's Google Play data disclosure.

Your choices about ads and statistics

  • Consent form. Where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland), the game shows Google's consent form, made with Google's User Messaging Platform, when it starts. No ad is requested and no statistics are collected until your choice allows it; if you do not agree, nothing is collected. The Analytics consent settings (analytics storage, ad storage, ad user data and ad personalisation) follow the choices you make in the form. One choice covers ads and statistics, and you can change or withdraw it at any time with Privacy choices for ads at the bottom of the game's CASH & EXTRAS screen (tap the "+" beside your money).
  • Where the law does not require consent, statistics are collected from the first start. Clearing the game's data or reinstalling it gives the game a new app instance ID that is not connected to the old one.
  • Personalised ads. If you do not agree to personalised ads, Google can still show ads that are not based on your past activity. These still use data such as the IP address for measurement and fraud prevention.
  • Advertising ID. You can reset or delete the Android advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalisation, in your phone's Android settings.
  • Remove ads. The optional "Remove ads" purchase stops the full-screen ads after closing. Rewarded ads stay available, and you only see one when you choose to.

Google Play Games: sign-in and cloud save

Fix & Foil uses Google Play Games Services, a service run by Google.

  • Sign-in. If you have a Play Games profile, Google Play Games may sign you in when the game starts. Signing in is optional: the game works without it. Google handles the sign-in. The game does not read, store or send your name, e-mail address, Play Games name, avatar or player ID.
  • Cloud save. While you are signed in, the game uploads a copy of the shop described above to Google Play Games ("Saved Games"), where it is stored in your own Google account. When the game finds a copy there that is further along than the one on your phone (after a reinstall or on another device), it asks you before loading it. This copy is sent over an encrypted connection. The developer cannot see or access it.
  • Google's own data. Google's Play Games software inside the game also collects analytics and diagnostics data for Google, to keep its service stable. See the Google Privacy Policy.
  • Not signed in. If you are not signed in to Google Play Games, nothing is uploaded and your shop stays only on your device.

Purchases

Optional purchases (shop money, a one-time Starter bundle, and Remove ads) are sold and processed entirely by Google Play. The developer never sees your payment details. The game asks Google Play to confirm each purchase before it delivers it. Purchases buy in-game items only; they have no value outside the game. Google handles purchase information under the Google Privacy Policy.

Information the developer collects

Nothing that identifies you. Fix & Foil has no sign-in of its own and does not ask for your name, e-mail address, contacts, location, photos, files, camera or microphone. What the developer sees are the gameplay statistics described above, as reports in Google Analytics, tied only to random IDs, never to your name or e-mail address. The game has no crash-reporting service, and the developer runs no server of its own that receives data from it.

Permissions

  • Internet and network state: used for ads, the consent form, gameplay statistics, purchases, the cloud save and Google Play's licence check. The shop itself plays on your phone and works offline.
  • Google Play Billing (com.android.vending.BILLING): shows prices and completes purchases with Google Play.
  • Advertising ID and Android ad services (com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID and the ACCESS_ADSERVICES_* permissions): added by Google's ads and Analytics software, so it can use the advertising ID and Android's ad measurement, as described under "Advertising" and "Gameplay statistics" above.
  • Vibration: haptic feedback while you restore things and open card packs.
  • Wake lock and audio settings: keep the screen on while you play and play sound and music.

Deleting your data

There is no account of the developer's, and the developer holds no data that identifies you.

  • The save on your device is deleted when you uninstall the game or clear its data in Android's settings.
  • The cloud save belongs to your Google account, and you can delete it yourself: open the Google Play Games app (or your Google Account's Play Games settings), go to Settings → Delete Play Games account & data, and delete the data for Fix & Foil. Uninstalling the game does not delete it.
  • Gameplay statistics are held by Google for the developer's Analytics account and deleted automatically after at most 14 months. They are tied only to the game's app instance ID; clearing the game's data or reinstalling it starts a new one.
  • Data collected by Google's ads software is held by Google. You can reset or delete the advertising ID in Android settings and use Google's own privacy tools through the Google Privacy Policy.
  • Purchase records are held by Google Play.

Children

Fix & Foil is meant for players aged 13 and over. It is not directed at children under 13, and the developer does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone. If you believe a child under 13 is playing and have a question about it, write to info@devcode.cloud.
